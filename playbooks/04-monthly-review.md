# Playbook 04: Monthly review

Goal: one page the owner reads in five minutes, with numbers from real sources only.

## Collect (label every number with its source)

| Metric | Source | Part |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue, jobs completed, average ticket | CRM reports | CRM |
| Leads by source, close rate by source | CRM pipeline | CRM |
| Ad spend, leads, CPL, CTR, frequency | Ads Manager / Google Ads | Ads (`/ads:report`) |
| Organic clicks, impressions, top pages, queries | Search Console, Bing | SEO ("Build the monthly report for <month>") |
| Reviews gained, average rating | Google Business Profile, CRM reviews | SEO + CRM |
| Videos published, best performer | God Mode Cut / Buffer | Video |

## Write `business/reports/<yyyy-mm>.md`

1. **Headline:** revenue vs last month and vs goal.
2. **Channels:** a table of leads, cost, close rate and revenue per source.
3. **What worked, what didn't** (three bullets each, with evidence).
4. **Next month:** at most five actions, each owned by one part, each marked "needs approval" when it spends money.
5. **Missing data:** list anything not connected instead of estimating it.

Update `BUSINESS.md` goals if the owner changes them.
